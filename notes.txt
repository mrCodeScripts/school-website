1. School Principal
    🚀 View school-wide reports (student performance, staff efficiency, financial reports).
    🚀 Approve/reject important decisions (budgets, policy changes, disciplinary actions).
    🚀 Manage the school calendar and events.
    🚀 Send announcements to staff, students, and parents.
2. Vice Principal/Assistant Principal
    🚀 Manage student discipline records (suspensions, warnings, behavior reports).
    🚀 Oversee teacher evaluations and classroom observations.
    🚀 Assist with scheduling classes and exams.
    🚀 Approve leave requests from teachers and staff.
3. School Administrator
    🚀 Manage user accounts (teachers, students, parents).
    🚀 Maintain student and staff records (admission, personal details, ID generation).
    🚀 Oversee enrollment and admissions processing.
    🚀 Generate and distribute official school documents (report cards, certificates, letters).
4. Registrar
    🚀 Process student enrollment and registration.
    🚀 Maintain academic records and update student profiles.
    🚀 Issue transcripts, report cards, and student certificates.
5. Admissions Officer
    🚀 Handle new student applications and document verification.
    🚀 Track and update the admission status of applicants.
    🚀 Respond to inquiries from parents and prospective students.
6. Guidance Counselor
    🚀 Manage student counseling sessions and appointment scheduling.
    🚀 Track student behavior reports and mental health concerns.
    🚀 Communicate with parents regarding student issues.
7. Student Affairs Coordinator
    🚀 Oversee extracurricular activities and student organizations.
    🚀 Manage student disciplinary actions and appeals.
    🚀 Track student participation in school events and programs.
8. Teachers (Subject Teachers/Class Advisors)
    🚀 Manage class schedules and lesson plans.
    🚀 Input and update student grades.
    🚀 Take attendance and monitor student participation.
    🚀 Send student progress reports to parents.
9. Department Heads
    🚀 Oversee subject-specific curriculum planning.
    🚀 Approve and manage teacher schedules.
    🚀 Evaluate teacher performance and provide feedback.
10. Librarian
    🚀 Manage book lending, returns, and inventory.
    🚀 Track overdue books and impose fines if necessary.
    🚀 Maintain a digital catalog of school resources.





Vice Principal Functionalities:
1️⃣ **Manage Student Discipline Records**  
   - Record student offenses (e.g., tardiness, misconduct, academic dishonesty)  
   - Issue warnings, suspensions, or detentions  
   - Notify parents about disciplinary actions via email/SMS  
   - Generate reports on student behavior trends  

2️⃣ **Oversee Teacher Evaluations**  
   - Schedule classroom observations for teachers  
   - Record performance feedback and improvement areas  
   - Approve or reject teacher evaluation reports  
   - Generate teacher performance analytics  

3️⃣ **Assist with Class and Exam Scheduling**  
   - Assign teachers to classes based on availability  
   - Create and modify timetables for students and staff  
   - Schedule exam dates and assign invigilators  
   - Prevent scheduling conflicts with automated checks  

4️⃣ **Approve Leave Requests from Teachers and Staff**  
   - Review and approve/reject leave requests  
   - Monitor leave balances and ensure adequate staffing  
   - Notify affected departments when a leave is granted  
   - Generate reports on staff leave trends  


Principal Functionalities  
1️⃣ **View School-Wide Reports**  
   - Access student academic performance reports  
   - View attendance statistics for students and teachers  
   - Monitor school financial reports (budget, expenses, revenue)  
   - Generate summary reports for board meetings  

2️⃣ **Approve/Reject Important Decisions**  
   - Review budget proposals and approve school expenditures  
   - Approve curriculum changes and policy updates  
   - Make final decisions on disciplinary actions for severe cases  
   - Approve major school events and partnerships  

3️⃣ **Manage School Calendar and Events**  
   - Schedule and oversee school-wide events (seminars, graduations, exams)  
   - Modify academic calendars and notify all users  
   - Approve department-specific events and activities  
   - Automate event reminders for students, teachers, and staff  

4️⃣ **Send Announcements to Staff, Students, and Parents**  
   - Broadcast important school updates and emergency alerts  
   - Send reminders for deadlines, holidays, and policy changes  
   - Notify parents about school performance and achievements  
   - Manage announcement history and allow scheduled posts  

School Administrator Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Manage User Accounts**  
   - Create and assign roles for students, teachers, and staff  
   - Update user details (contact info, role changes, profile updates)  
   - Reset passwords and handle account security issues  
   - Deactivate accounts for graduated students or resigned staff  

2️⃣ **Maintain Student and Staff Records**  
   - Store and manage personal details (name, address, contact info)  
   - Update records for student promotions, transfers, and graduations  
   - Track employment records for teachers and staff  
   - Ensure data security and access control for sensitive information  

3️⃣ **Oversee Enrollment and Admissions Processing**  
   - Monitor admission applications and verify submitted documents  
   - Approve/reject enrollment requests based on school policies  
   - Generate student IDs and assign unique registration numbers  
   - Send enrollment confirmation notifications to students and parents  

4️⃣ **Generate and Distribute Official School Documents**  
   - Issue report cards, certificates, and recommendation letters  
   - Provide employment verification letters for staff  
   - Generate school fee receipts and invoices  
   - Track document requests and automate processing times  


Registrar Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Process Student Enrollment and Registration**  
   - Handle new student applications and validate required documents  
   - Assign student numbers and class sections upon registration  
   - Monitor available slots for each grade level and program  
   - Generate registration status reports for the admin and principal  

2️⃣ **Maintain Academic Records**  
   - Store and update student grades, attendance, and disciplinary records  
   - Track student progress across different academic years  
   - Ensure accurate record-keeping for transcripts and graduation eligibility  
   - Provide historical academic data when needed  

3️⃣ **Issue Transcripts, Report Cards, and Student Certificates**  
   - Generate and distribute student report cards each term  
   - Process transcript requests for graduates and transfers  
   - Issue certificates for academic achievements and extracurricular awards  
   - Automate document requests and reduce manual workload  

Admissions Officer Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Handle New Student Applications**  
   - Receive and review admission applications  
   - Validate submitted documents (birth certificates, previous school records)  
   - Assign application status (pending, approved, rejected)  
   - Communicate application updates to parents and students  

2️⃣ **Track and Update Admission Status**  
   - Monitor the number of available slots per grade level/program  
   - Assign students to sections after approval  
   - Maintain a waitlist for qualified applicants when slots are full  
   - Generate admission status reports for school administrators  

3️⃣ **Respond to Inquiries from Parents and Prospective Students**  
   - Provide details on admission requirements and deadlines  
   - Schedule and manage entrance exams/interviews  
   - Send automated email/SMS notifications for admission updates  
   - Offer support for scholarship and financial aid applications  


Guidance Counselor Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Manage Student Counseling Sessions**  
   - Schedule and track one-on-one or group counseling sessions  
   - Maintain confidential records of student concerns and progress  
   - Assign intervention plans for students with academic or behavioral issues  
   - Provide referrals to specialists if needed  

2️⃣ **Track Student Behavior and Mental Health Concerns**  
   - Record student discipline reports and interventions  
   - Monitor student emotional well-being and provide necessary support  
   - Identify students at risk of dropping out and implement prevention measures  
   - Work closely with teachers and parents on student development  

3️⃣ **Communicate with Parents Regarding Student Issues**  
   - Notify parents about student behavioral concerns and recommendations  
   - Provide updates on student emotional or psychological support  
   - Organize parent meetings for counseling-related discussions  
   - Maintain records of parental involvement and feedback  

Student Affairs Coordinator Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Oversee Extracurricular Activities and Student Organizations**  
   - Approve and manage student clubs and organizations  
   - Schedule and oversee school events, competitions, and trips  
   - Track student participation in extracurricular activities  
   - Allocate school resources for student activities  

2️⃣ **Manage Student Disciplinary Actions**  
   - Record and track disciplinary cases involving students  
   - Approve student appeals for disciplinary decisions  
   - Coordinate with guidance counselors for behavioral interventions  
   - Generate reports on student behavior trends and incidents  

3️⃣ **Track Student Participation in School Events**  
   - Maintain attendance records for school events and programs  
   - Assign leadership roles and responsibilities to students in activities  
   - Ensure that participation records reflect on student reports  
   - Generate activity participation certificates  

Teacher Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Manage Class Schedules and Lesson Plans**  
   - Create and update lesson plans for assigned subjects  
   - Manage daily, weekly, or monthly class schedules  
   - Assign homework, projects, and reading materials  
   - Modify lesson schedules based on academic progress  

2️⃣ **Input and Update Student Grades**  
   - Record grades for quizzes, exams, and assignments  
   - Generate progress reports for students and parents  
   - Handle grade corrections and justification requests  
   - Ensure grade calculations align with school policies  

3️⃣ **Take Attendance and Monitor Student Participation**  
   - Record daily student attendance per class  
   - Track tardiness and absences with automatic notifications  
   - Monitor student engagement and participation in activities  
   - Generate attendance reports for parents and administrators  

4️⃣ **Send Student Progress Reports to Parents**  
   - Provide individual student performance updates  
   - Send personalized feedback on academic strengths and weaknesses  
   - Notify parents about upcoming tests, assignments, and deadlines  
   - Maintain communication logs for parent interactions  

Department Head Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Oversee Subject-Specific Curriculum Planning**  
   - Design and approve curriculum guidelines for subjects  
   - Ensure alignment with government education standards  
   - Review and update textbooks and learning materials  
   - Coordinate with teachers for curriculum improvements  

2️⃣ **Approve and Manage Teacher Schedules**  
   - Assign class loads and teaching responsibilities  
   - Balance teacher workload to prevent scheduling conflicts  
   - Approve class rescheduling or adjustments when necessary  
   - Ensure faculty compliance with academic deadlines  

3️⃣ **Evaluate Teacher Performance and Provide Feedback**  
   - Conduct classroom observations and performance reviews  
   - Provide recommendations for teacher development and training  
   - Address teacher concerns and escalate major issues to the principal  
   - Track teacher progress and generate performance reports  

Librarian Functionalities  
1️⃣ **Manage Book Lending, Returns, and Inventory**  
   - Issue books to students and staff and track due dates  
   - Process book returns and apply fines for late submissions  
   - Maintain a real-time library inventory system  
   - Add new books and remove outdated materials  

2️⃣ **Track Overdue Books and Impose Fines**  
   - Send overdue reminders to students and staff  
   - Implement a fine system for lost or damaged books  
   - Restrict borrowing privileges for repeat offenders  
   - Generate overdue book reports for school administration  

3️⃣ **Maintain a Digital Catalog of School Resources**  
   - Digitize and organize a searchable book database  
   - Categorize books by subject, author, and availability status  
   - Allow students to reserve books online  
   - Provide access to e-books and digital research materials
